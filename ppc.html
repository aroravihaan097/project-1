📊 Production Possibility Curve Analysis

Authored by Vihaan Arora

1. The Core Concept

The Production Possibility Curve (PPC) represents the maximum combination of two goods an economy can produce given fixed resources and technology. It highlights the concept of opportunity cost.


2. Government Policy Impacts

When the government implements structural policies (like "Make in India" or skill development programs), it actively shifts the PPC outwards due to technological advancement and resource optimization.


Key Metric: A rightward shift signifies economic growth, while movement *along* the curve implies reallocation of existing resources without total capacity expansion.
